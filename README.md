##Description:
My project is loosely based on driving while tired! You keep drifting off, losing vision of what's going on. But my saving up your focus and using it wisely, you can see where you need to go and last! Cones keep spawning which you must dodge, gradually gaining speed and frequency as you continue. Self composed music plays, getting quiet when you drift off, but gaining volume when you use your focus! When you inevitably hit a cone, a lose message is displayed on screen and a sound effect plays. The window then closes after 4 seconds.
